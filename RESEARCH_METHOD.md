# Evidence method

For each material claim, identify a primary source where possible, inspect it, and link the specific page. Preserve the source's jurisdiction, date and scope. Separate facts from examples, hypotheses and forecasts. A retrieved passage is material to evaluate, not automatically reliable evidence.

Record contradictions and missing information. Do not cite a model response as proof. Use synthetic data for public exercises; retain unchanged inputs and document transformations. Report negative results and failed checks. For changing model catalogs and software setup, recheck official documentation before a release.

This edition uses concise paraphrases and original exercises. It is not a systematic review, professional guideline or verified statement that every external link will remain available. Submit corrections with the passage, supporting source and proposed wording.
