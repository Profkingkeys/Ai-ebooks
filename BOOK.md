# The Book Map

## Working title

**AI: The New Operating Layer**

The central idea:

> AI is becoming a programmable layer between human intent and digital action.

Once that layer can read, reason, call tools, write software, operate workflows and consume private data, the interesting engineering question is no longer only "How good is the model?"

It becomes:

**What can the system do, what is it allowed to do, what can fool it, what can verify it, and what happens when it is wrong?**
